Rule 1: live state is read, not remembered

A claim about a live system is valid only in the turn it was read. Claude re-reads before saying a campaign is paused, a draft or sending.

Rule 2: confirm at the source

Vendor counters are not delivery. Outcomes are checked where the work was done, for example RSVPs for calendar invites.

Rule 3: procedure lives in code

Anything that says "remember to" is a latent bug, so the tools enforce it: correct dates, balanced sending, stamp only after ready.

5Automate it from chat

Eleven workflows you run by typing

Each card shows what to type, what Claude does behind the scenes, and what you get back. Blue steps run on their own; orange steps stop and wait for your go or send now; purple steps are yours.

First: where do the leads come from?

A wave can only invite people who are already in the Master Outreach Sheet. The sheet has one tab per webinar, and each lead is stamped once invited. So every send starts with a lead question. There are three routes, and they differ in what chat can do on its own today.

Works in chat today

Route A · Use the pool already in the sheet

Ask: "Is there enough unused list for another wave?" Claude reads each tab's stamped and unstamped counts and says yes or no. If yes, it goes straight to the wave plan.

  • Skips anyone stamped on any tab
  • Skips anyone already contacted in Tantra or Instantly
  • Skips RSVPs, attendees, suppressed people and blocked domains
  • Skips excluded countries and job-title words

You get: the exact count available and the plan card for the wave.

Chat + a person

Route B · A new list from Susanne or the team

Attach the file in the chat and ask Claude to check it. Claude can count rows, find duplicate and missing emails, spot non-financial industries and wrong countries, and flag other clients' contacts. It then writes a precise brief for the operations team.

  • Operations cleans it with the standard lead pipeline: de-duplicate by email, keep the nine financial industries, remove engaged and suppressed people, re-attach LinkedIn
  • Operations loads the cleaned rows into the webinar tab
  • Back in chat, sheet_tabs shows the new unstamped count and the wave can be planned

You get: a checked list, a ready brief, and a loaded tab.

Works in chat today

Route C · Cold email from any sheet

For Instantly only, the leads can sit in any Google Sheet tab with a header row, shared with the connector's Google account. Ask: "Preview the leads in this tab for the cold-email campaign."

  • Claude removes suppressed, blocked and previously contacted people
  • You see the plan, then say go to load, in batches of 1,000

You get: a clean Instantly campaign without touching the master sheet.

Can she upload a list in chat and have it added to the master sheet? Not yet. No connector tool writes lead rows into the sheet today, which is why loading is a person's step in Route B. Chat can check the list and create the empty tab, but the rows are loaded by operations.

Option to build: a sheet_add_leads tool. She would attach the list and say "add this to the master sheet and create the campaign". Claude would clean it, show a plan card (rows in, rows removed and why), load the tab after "go", then plan the wave. It would need building and testing, so it goes through the plan-card process first. Also possible: a search over the whole lead pool, so chat could pick the best unused leads without a hand-off.
"Go through all the lead lists we have": the full set of collated lists is held in the operations workspace, where it is cleaned and filtered. Chat cannot open those files directly. Today it sees the result through the sheet tabs. A pool-search tool, above, would let chat go through the whole pool itself.

Her voice, checked every time

Always
  • First person, friendly and knowledgeable
  • Hook in the first one or two lines
  • One idea per line, short sentences
  • One call to action, and end on it
  • Webinar link in the first comment
  • British English
Never
  • Hype words, manufactured urgency, "comment and I'll DM"
  • Anything negative, critical or controversial
  • Invented statistics, clients or quotes
  • A closing question to the reader
  • Naming the listed bank, or investment advice
  • Another client's content or claims

9Call plan and notes

What we cover on the call

Keep in mind

  • Ask for numbers any time; they are read live, not remembered.
  • Anything that reaches people waits for a typed "send now".
  • The webinar email drafts in GHL stay unpublished.
  • Blocking an address is not erasing it. Deletion requests also need the row removed.
  • Susanne signs off every post before it is scheduled.

Good first things to try

  • "What is the next webinar, and how many have registered?"
  • "Is there enough unused list for another wave?"
  • "Show the plan for the next invite wave."
  • "Write a framework carousel on becoming board-ready."
  • "Check this draft against the approval checklist."

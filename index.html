Part 3 · Examples

Four tasks Claude can do today

1 · Send a webinar invite wave

"Plan the next wave for the upcoming webinar."

  1. Check the broadcast exists for the event date, or create it after a go.
  2. Check there are enough unused leads in the Master Outreach Sheet.
  3. Show the plan card: counts, copy variation, schedule, geo mix, skipped leads.
  4. On "send now", create the campaigns and confirm each one is ready.
  5. Mark only the confirmed leads as used, so nobody is invited twice.

2 · Launch the cold email for an event

"Set up the Instantly campaign for the next webinar."

  1. Read the latest campaign and propose the date and time replacements.
  2. Copy it as a new draft. It refuses if any old date or time is left in the copy.
  3. Preview leads, with suppressed, blocked and previously contacted people removed.
  4. Load the leads after a go, then review the draft together.
  5. Start sending only on "send now".

3 · Ask for the numbers

Ask ClaudeIt reads
How many have said yes or maybe to the next webinar?Calendar RSVPs (Tantra)
Who registered and who actually attended?WebinarGeek attendance
How is each cold-email campaign performing?Instantly analytics
What is revenue, and by which tier?GoHighLevel payments
Is there enough fresh list for another wave?Master Outreach Sheet

4 · Keep the list clean

"This company replied asking to be removed. Block them everywhere."

What Claude does

  • Blocks the whole domain in Instantly
  • Marks every record at that domain as unsubscribed in Tantra
  • Adds it to the shared suppression list

Built-in protection

  • Every lead preview already removes suppressed people
  • Never blocks a free email provider or our own sending domains
  • Replies are classified before any wave
Blocking is not erasure. A GDPR deletion request still needs the row removed from the sheets and a reply sent.

Part 3 · Runs by itself

What happens after someone registers

Email: WebinarGeek

  • Confirmation
  • Reminders before the webinar
  • Follow-up and replay link

Everyone gets this, with or without a phone number.

WhatsApp: GoHighLevel

  • Join link when they message "send me my join link"
  • Four timed reminders before the broadcast

Only for registrants who gave a phone number.

A Cloudflare Worker keeps GoHighLevel up to date from WebinarGeek (registered, attended, watched replay, no-show), so WhatsApp and tags always match reality. WebinarGeek owns email, GHL owns WhatsApp, and the two never overlap. Detail: knowledge file 19.

Part 4 · Content workflow

From idea to scheduled post

  1. BriefSusanne or the team: format, topic, source material, call to action, date.
  2. Claude draftsPost copy in her voice, first comment, slide-by-slide text, design brief.
  3. Claude checksRuns the approval checklist and fixes anything that fails.
  4. Susanne signs offEvery single post, recorded in the Content Calendar tab.
  5. Design and scheduleFinished in the named template, scheduled in Metricool or LinkedIn.

Ten approved formats

4 image formats

  • Experiential: a real moment turned into a lesson
  • Thought leadership: one sharp point of view
  • Quotes: one quotable line
  • Cheatsheet: a dense, save-worthy reference

6 carousel formats

  • Framework deck: a step-by-step method
  • Comparison deck: old thinking against new
  • Checklist deck: a practical list
  • Illustrated story
  • Book or resource list
  • Case study, real and permissioned only

Written as Susanne, checked every time

Always

  • First person, friendly and knowledgeable
  • Hook in the first two lines
  • One idea per line, short sentences
  • One call to action, and end on it
  • Webinar link in the first comment

Never

  • Hype words, urgency, or "comment and I'll DM"
  • Anything negative, critical or controversial
  • Invented statistics, clients or quotes
  • A closing question to the reader
  • Naming the listed bank, or investment advice
